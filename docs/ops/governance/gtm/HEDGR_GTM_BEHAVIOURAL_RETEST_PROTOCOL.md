**Ticket / authority:** `GTM-MARKET-DISCOVERY-001`, cycle 2; D-151 / HEDGR_STATUS.md §6f.22 / §7 / §7a / §284. Protocol prepared 2026-09-19. This is a fixed execution instrument under the approved record, not collected evidence.

**Purpose:** Test whether freelancer and salaried comparison groups share a recurring, consequential unresolved stability decision. Ghana, Kenya and the Philippines are contexts. Kenya is the clarity case; no country selection or ranking. Freelancers remain the anchor; salaried inclusion changes no canonical audience. No product demonstration or financial execution is needed for this episode study.

## 1. Fixed scope and sampling

These are conservative operator choices that operationalise the approved decision; the Founder did not specify numerical sample sizes or age bounds in the supplied record.

- At most 12 eligible responses analysed: up to two freelancers and two salaried comparison participants in each of Ghana, Kenya and the Philippines. Examine freelancer evidence first; Kenya provides a first clarity comparison, not preferential market status. Do not transfer unused cell capacity to another country/cohort. Report missing cells.
- Adults only. Freelancer: already earns recurring independent income, uses digital financial tools and has agency over spending/retaining/converting/allocation. Comparison: salaried, age 18–35 inclusive, recurring earned salary, regular digital banking/wallet use, some surplus/buffer/savings capacity after essentials and agency over repeated money decisions. No precise age, employer or income amount is collected. Age 18–35 is an operational definition for this reversible study, not canonical audience policy.
- Do not require foreign-currency income for every respondent. Note income/currency structure only if volunteered without identifying details; retain the freelancer/cross-currency hypothesis as a hypothesis, not a recruitment success condition. Do not exclude an otherwise eligible person because their latest decision was easy or existing tools were sufficient.
- Dominant lack of income/access, unemployment/income creation, credit need, debt distress, general remittance receipt, comprehensive budgeting, broad financial education or investment-management needs are outside this population. Do not solicit detailed explanations of ineligibility or recruit adjacent groups to fill gaps.
- Use only an existing permissioned private channel managed by the Founder. No scraped contacts, public advertisements, incentives/spend, provider/counsel contact or other-lane recruitment/evidence. Willingness must be independent of employment, credit, service access or any promised financial benefit.
- One response per person, requested without sign-in/email collection. Anonymous collection cannot fully enforce uniqueness; flag suspected duplication without fingerprinting or identifiers. Analyse the earliest eligible consented responses in each cell, regardless of whether they support the thesis; no cherry-picking or replacement of negative cases.
- At most 24 consent/eligibility submissions; close intake at that cap, when all 12 eligible slots are filled, at an earlier stop/insufficiency finding, or 21 calendar days after the activation merge, whichever comes first. Founder records the activation date and calculated closure date before opening. Manual Form closure may admit overflow: quarantine it without analysis and delete it under the raw-response controls. Do not extend the cycle to compensate for unavailable participants.
- One synthesis follows collection. A missing country/cohort or insufficient narrative is a finding about evidence adequacy, not evidence that the behaviour does not exist.

### Section 2 — Research profile

Description:

For this small study, the freelancer profile means you are 18 or older, already receive recurring earned income from freelance or independent work, use digital financial tools, and make your own money decisions. The salaried profile means you are 18–35, receive recurring salary, regularly use digital bank or wallet tools, retain some surplus, buffer or savings capacity after essentials, and can act on your repeated spending or saving decisions. Your latest decision does not need to have been difficult.

Please choose “None of these / prefer not to say” if your main financial need is income creation, basic financial access, credit or debt-distress support, general remittance receipt, comprehensive budgeting, financial education or investment management. ### Section 3 — Recent decisions

Description: Please use your own recent experience, without checking or opening financial accounts. Broad descriptions are enough. Written questions are optional. “Nothing was unclear”, “nothing changed” and “I have no example” are useful answers.

**Q03 — Recent event** (multiple choice; required)

“Thinking about the last three months, which event is most relevant to your most recent decision about money you could use?”

- “Salary became available”
- “Freelance / client payment became available”
- “An upcoming bill or purchase”
- “A change in a currency exchange rate”
- “Another event”
- “No relevant event or decision in the last three months”
- “Prefer not to say”

**Q04 — The episode** (paragraph; optional)

“What happened, and what, if anything, did you need to decide? Describe the options you actually considered. Please do not include exact amounts, dates or identifying details.”

**Q05 — Existing method** (paragraph; optional)

“How did you decide what to do? Describe the tools, information, personal rules or people you relied on. Generic descriptions such as bank app, wallet or spreadsheet are enough.”

**Q06 — What was unresolved** (paragraph; optional)

“After using those methods, what, if anything, was still unclear? If you already knew what to do, please say so. What made the decision easy or difficult?”

**Q07 — Actual consequence** (paragraph; optional)

“What did you actually do next? Did anything you learned change, delay or confirm your action? Describe what made the difference, or say that nothing changed.”

**Q08 — Recurrence and counterexample** (paragraph; optional)

“Has a similar decision happened again in the last three months? Describe another occasion if you can, including one that was easier or did not need extra help. If this was a one-off or you cannot recall another example, please say so.”

**Q09 — Before a commitment** (paragraph; optional)

“Separately, recall the most recent meaningful financial commitment you considered in the last three months. How did you decide whether you could go ahead, and what did you actually do? If it was the same episode, no extra decision was needed, or you have no example, say so.”

**Q10 — Existing alternatives** (paragraph; optional)

“What do your existing tools or habits already do well enough for these decisions? What, if anything, remains missing? Would that missing part mainly help you decide, help you carry out a decision you had already made, or something else?”

**Q11 — Possible added information** (paragraph; optional)

“If any information was missing, what would you have needed to know? How, if at all, might it have affected your action? This is a possibility, not a claim about something that happened.”

**Q12 — Contrary evidence** (paragraph; optional)

“What would make extra help with these decisions unnecessary or unhelpful? Is there anything in your experience that the questions above have missed?”

## 5. Analysis and falsification rules

Keep the two cohorts separate until within-cohort analysis is complete. Code each available episode in private de-identified working evidence for trigger, existing method, unresolved interpretation, actual reported action, consequence, recurrence/counterexample and substitute sufficiency. Do not impute missing text or infer uncertainty from income structure. The second episode checks recurrence; a vague “often” does not establish frequency or an observed episode. No-event and no-gap responses are usable negative evidence, not failed recruitment.

Tag claims as **retrospective self-report**, **hypothetical response**, **analyst inference**, or **unknown**. This self-guided instrument supplies no observed financial action, interviewer observation, verified account record, product test or causal Hedgr effect. Agreement that information might help is weaker than a reported episode where interpretation changed an action. The study can support a recurring-problem hypothesis while leaving Hedgr's incremental effectiveness unproven; report that explicitly.

Compare the decision's trigger, information gap, action consequence, recurrence and current alternative across cohorts before interpreting country or rail context. Support the common grammar only where actual episode reports align, not merely common vocabulary. Income variability and cross-currency differences may be explanatory conditions; record material differences instead of forcing them into one model.

Seek and retain these disconfirming patterns: decision already settled and execution is missing; cheaper/faster transfers; dominant FX timing; salaried budgeting need; sufficient banking/wallet support; no consequential role for interpretation; different core logic; infrequent events. Consistent negative patterns trigger early narrowing/stop and synthesis under §7a; they do not authorize new cohorts, extra questions, more countries or unbounded responses.

The one later evidence file must contain: method and coverage (including access/missing-data limits); non-identifying cross-cohort comparison; supporting and contrary episode patterns; existing-alternative analysis; limits on incremental-Hedgr claims; consent/protocol deviations and exclusions; one recommended terminal disposition with uncertainty; and the explicit distinction from the actual Founder decision. No country ranking, numerical product score, inferred population prevalence or qualitative-saturation claim.

The four Founder outcomes are **RETAIN**, **BROADEN**, **SPLIT**, **STOP / REVISE**. RETAIN needs materially stronger freelancer/cross-currency evidence; BROADEN warrants only separate consideration of a common audience; SPLIT requires meaningful but different grammars; STOP / REVISE includes failure to establish sufficient consequence, recurrence or incremental value. Lack of evidence must not be mislabelled as falsification. No outcome activates product work or another cycle. Final Founder disposition is recorded in HEDGR_STATUS.md §284, not fabricated in the evidence output.
