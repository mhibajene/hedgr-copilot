# Founder / HedgrOps Disposition — Working MVP Audience Hypothesis

**Decision Type:** GTM / Product Convergence
**Status:** Founder Approved — reconciled by HEDGR_STATUS.md §285; operative on permanent-main merge
**Visibility:** Founder Only
**Decision Lane:** Strategic / reversible product hypothesis
**Financial Execution Impact:** None
**MVP Gate Created:** No

---

## Disposition

**ADOPT WORKING MVP AUDIENCE HYPOTHESIS — DEFER FORMAL BEHAVIOURAL-MARKET RETEST**

Hedgr will proceed into MVP productisation on the working hypothesis that its initial behavioural market comprises:

> **Financially active earners — initially freelancers, independent professionals and salaried professionals — who receive recurring income, possess sufficient financial agency to make allocation decisions, and want a more intelligent way to preserve and improve financial stability.**

This is a **working product hypothesis**, not a claim of demonstrated market fit or a permanent target-market definition.

---

## Shared Product Hypothesis

The common job is not primarily money movement.

The working hypothesis is:

> **Users benefit from a stability-intelligence layer that helps them understand what available money should do — what is committed, what should remain liquid, what is exposed, and what can safely be spent, retained, converted or allocated — before taking financial action.**

Freelancers and salaried professionals may differ in income structure and financial rails while still sharing the same underlying Stability Engine decision grammar.

This proposition will be tested through observed MVP behaviour rather than treated as proven in advance.

---

## Rationale

The first Lane G market-discovery cycle did not produce sufficient evidence to select Ghana, Kenya or the Philippines as a launch market.

It did, however, expose a more valuable uncertainty:

> Whether Hedgr's differentiated job is helping users interpret financial stability decisions rather than merely helping them move foreign income.

Pre-MVP behavioural research can provide useful directional evidence, but the Founder judges that stronger evidence is likely to emerge once users can experience a sufficiently coherent Hedgr MVP and respond to it in realistic financial contexts.

Accordingly, behavioural-market validation will **not gate current MVP productisation**.

The intended progression becomes:

> **Working hypothesis → coherent MVP → real user exposure → observed behaviour → behavioural-market validation → retain / revise / narrow**

rather than:

> working hypothesis → prove market in advance → productise.

This preserves learning velocity while keeping the hypothesis reversible.

---

## MVP Audience Boundary

The working audience includes users who:

- receive recurring earned income;
- already use digital financial tools;
- have some meaningful ability to retain, save or allocate money;
- face recurring decisions about spending, liquidity, exposure or allocation;
- can act on financial insight.

Initial manifestations include:

- freelancers;
- independent professionals;
- salaried young professionals and professionals.

The behavioural boundary is more important than employment category.

## Productisation Implication

MVP development should target the **shared financial grammar**, not separate persona-specific feature trees.

The core experience should help a user understand:

1. **What do I have?**
2. **What is already committed?**
3. **What should remain liquid?**
4. **What is changing or exposed?**
5. **What can I safely do next?**

No separate freelancer mode or salaried-professional mode is justified without later evidence of materially different decision logic.

The Stability Engine remains the product centre.

The wallet remains the experience / eventual execution surface.

Financial rails remain enabling infrastructure.

---

## Deferred Behavioural Retest

The previously proposed formal freelancer-versus-professional behavioural-market retest is **deferred, not cancelled**.

It should resume once users can experience a sufficiently coherent MVP and stronger behavioural evidence becomes available.

The retest should then use observed interaction and real financial context to determine whether freelancers and salaried professionals genuinely share one behavioural market.

This deferred validation is not a prerequisite for current MVP productisation.

---

## MVP Learning Criteria

Once users begin experiencing Hedgr, evidence should be collected against the following questions:

### Trigger
What causes the user to open Hedgr?

### Comprehension
Does the user understand their stability position and what the product is communicating?

### Interpretation Value
Does Hedgr provide useful understanding that existing banks, wallets or financial tools do not?

### Decision Impact
Does the resulting interpretation materially influence an actual financial decision?

### Recurrence
Does another meaningful financial event cause the user to return?

### Cohort Convergence
Do freelancers and salaried professionals follow the same underlying reasoning journey?

### Failure Mode
Is the dominant unmet need actually:

- budgeting;
- transfer execution;
- cheaper payouts;
- FX timing;
- another job outside the Stability Engine thesis?

---

## Evidence That Strengthens the Hypothesis

The working audience thesis strengthens where observed behaviour shows that:

- users have money available but lack confidence about allocation;
- existing tools show balances or transaction options without adequately explaining consequences;
- users create informal liquidity or buffer rules;
- users reconcile information across multiple accounts or tools;
- users delay actions while figuring out what is financially safe;
- Hedgr's interpretation changes or clarifies a decision;
- users return when another financial decision occurs;
- different income structures produce materially similar stability reasoning.

A particularly strong signal would be:

> **Different users, income structures and rails — same underlying stability decision grammar.**

---

## Evidence That Weakens or Narrows the Hypothesis

The hypothesis should be revised or narrowed if:

- freelancer needs resolve mainly to payout or FX execution;
- salaried users primarily require conventional budgeting;
- users already understand their financial position sufficiently;
- Hedgr interpretation does not materially affect behaviour;
- users do not return around subsequent decisions;
- the cohorts require substantially different product logic;
- recurring stability decisions are too infrequent to support a meaningful product relationship.

Negative evidence must lead to narrowing or revision rather than audience expansion.

---

## Market Implication

Ghana, Kenya and the Philippines remain market hypotheses.

No country is selected by this disposition.

Market selection should occur only after stronger evidence exists regarding:

- recurring user behaviour;
- interpretation value;
- repeat use;
- cohort fit;
- later infrastructure operability.

Infrastructure remains necessary but subordinate to demonstrated product relevance.

Working heuristic:

> **Follow the decision first; then determine the liquidity, rails and market conditions required to execute it.**

This is a working GTM interpretation, not canonical doctrine.

No additional research cycle is automatically authorised by this disposition.
