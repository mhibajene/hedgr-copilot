- Each stage explicitly denied evidence acceptance, registry movement, provider outreach, implementation, and customer-money activity.
- The Kenya and M-Pesa framing became more precise without being promoted into market or endpoint selection.

- The synthesis's decision-support language can be mistaken for sequencing unless its non-authoritative posture is retained.
