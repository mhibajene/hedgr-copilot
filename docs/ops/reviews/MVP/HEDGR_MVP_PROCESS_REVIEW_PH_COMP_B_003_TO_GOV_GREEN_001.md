The slice is assessed against four existing Hedgr concerns:

## 6. Process assessment

What changed:

- Previously classified Philippines source candidates gained exact, inspectable public locators and metadata boundaries.
- The synthetic withdrawal research surface gained stronger fixture traceability, safe-exit behavior, state-specific prompts, and verified non-mutation constraints.
- MVP evidence domains gained a common, populated schema that exposed missing evidence instead of converting artifact presence into acceptance.
- Founder outcome selection became reconstructible as a bounded decision record with an explicit synthetic research boundary.
- Green Lane delegation gained repo-native controls separating classification and preparation from ticket activation and implementation.

What did not change:

- The system remained Class A / informational and non-executing; no Class B or Class C capability was established.
- No Evidence Register domain was accepted and no institutional convergence gate was satisfied.
- No market, provider, endpoint, rail, chain, stablecoin, custody model, legal position, or settlement path was selected or approved.
- No participant-comprehension result was produced by prototype hardening or facilitator verification.
- No live deposit, withdrawal, conversion, payout, refund, reconciliation, or customer-money path was introduced.
- Neither an outcome disposition nor a delegation envelope independently supplied implementation or sequencing authority.

Process limitations:

- Exact source locators improve reproducibility but do not establish source currentness, applicability, legal effect, or evidentiary sufficiency.
- A well-bounded prototype can improve research integrity while leaving participant interpretation unknown.
- Schema-complete evidence records can make absence legible while all material domains remain incomplete.
- A recorded outcome hypothesis improves decision traceability but does not prove the hypothesis.
- Delegation controls improve authority clarity but can be over-read if their expiry, explicit activation requirement, or current retirement is omitted.
- The slice is contiguous in completed-ticket order but spans evidence hygiene, a synthetic product surface, evidence governance, outcome governance, and operator delegation; it is not one integrated capability milestone.
