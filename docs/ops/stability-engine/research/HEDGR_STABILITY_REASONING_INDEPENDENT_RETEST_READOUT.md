## 5. Named uncertainty readout

### F1 / F2 boundary

The boundary held across the critical controls:

- BR-02 improved F2 without changing F1 when a challenged claim recovered near nominal value.
- BR-07 worsened F2 without changing F1 when a transferable claim suffered a material nominal haircut while runway was held constant.
- BR-08 worsened F1 without changing F2 when redemption was delayed beyond the required horizon without a nominal haircut.

No reviewer collapsed access or exit timing into nominal integrity, and no new rule was required. This supports independent interpretability of the pre-D-099 F1/F2 distinction for these explicit cases. It does not test or dispose the different semantic direction recorded by D-099.

### Qualitative materiality

All reviewers applied the declared qualitative materiality without introducing a numeric threshold. BR-01's explicit lack of dominance led to abstention; BR-05's below-sufficiency runway condition supported the stated F1 impairment. Because the scenarios supplied the materiality classifications, this run does not establish that reviewers can independently classify borderline materiality from raw or incomplete evidence.

### Critical-function dominance

BR-05, BR-07, and BR-08 produced consistent application of the fixed F1/F2 critical-function rule. Reviewers kept non-critical improvements visible, did not offset them, and did not introduce a hidden weight. This demonstrates rule reproducibility within the instrument; it does not validate the rule as policy, architecture, or product truth.

### Abstention

Abstention was consistent and bounded:

- BR-01 — opposing non-dominant F3/F4 changes;
- BR-03 — unknown systemic-stress transmission;
- BR-06 — stale and conflicting critical F1 evidence; and
- BR-09 — missing FX transmission evidence for F3.

Reviewers also avoided over-abstention in BR-08, where current coherent evidence independently verified a critical F1 impairment, and in BR-10, where reliable evidence established no relevant transmission.

### Systemic-overlay transmission

The three-way control held: unknown pathways produced abstention in BR-03; a verified redemption pathway worsened F1 in BR-08; and verified non-transmission produced unchanged functions in BR-10. No reviewer treated systemic stress as a fifth score or a direction setter by itself.

### Stale and conflicting evidence

All reviewers left F1 unresolved in BR-06. None treated normal settlement as proof that redemption was functioning, and none converted conflicting evidence into deterioration without current coherent proof. The run does not establish freshness or reconciliation thresholds because none were supplied or authorised.
