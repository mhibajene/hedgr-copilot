## Observation

Recent convergence suggests that Hedgr's most meaningful product improvements have not come from adding explanation. They have come from delaying explanation until it becomes meaningful.

Earlier iterations attempted to communicate constitutional truth as early as possible. Graduated Fidelity suggests a different institutional behaviour: reveal only what the participant is ready to integrate into a correct mental model, while preserving the complete truth and making deeper reasoning available when it becomes useful.

The institution increasingly exercises restraint.

---

## Founder interpretation

Disciplined Restraint is not an endorsement of minimalism, informational reduction, or weaker disclosure.

The institution should reveal understanding progressively rather than reveal information immediately. A participant should not receive more institutional reasoning than is required for the next correct understanding.

The objective is epistemic discipline: the institution remains truthful and complete while taking responsibility for when each layer of reasoning can genuinely support understanding.

---

## Enduring institutional learning

> The institution demonstrates wisdom not by revealing everything it knows, but by revealing only what is required for the participant's next correct understanding.

This is an observation about institutional behaviour. It informs judgement; it does not prescribe implementation.

---

## Supporting observations

### 1. Information and understanding are different

More information does not necessarily produce greater understanding. Understanding compounds when each layer prepares the participant for the next.

### 2. Explanation has a natural moment

Explanation should appear when it answers a question the participant has naturally reached, not merely because the institution already possesses the answer.

### 3. Progressive understanding requires restraint

The institution should resist exposing internal structure, numerical representation, constitutional machinery, or implementation vocabulary until those concepts support understanding rather than compete with it.

### 4. The participant should not be rushed ahead of their own reasoning

Graduated Fidelity increasingly suggests that curiosity can create durable understanding, while premature explanation can create cognitive burden.

Progressive understanding is not achieved by revealing everything the institution knows. It is achieved by exercising disciplined restraint until the participant is ready for the next correct understanding.
