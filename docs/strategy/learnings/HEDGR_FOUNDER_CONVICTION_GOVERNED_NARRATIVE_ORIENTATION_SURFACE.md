## Founder conviction

Hedgr should develop a **reusable, governed narrative orientation capability** rather than continue producing disposable explanatory collateral for each testing or communication cycle.

The immediate opportunity is to express the existing Hedgr narrative as a simple web-native orientation surface. The institutional objective, however, is broader than creating an “About page.”

The objective is to establish a durable pattern through which Hedgr can:

- maintain a coherent narrative source;
- preserve provenance between governed narrative and rendered surfaces;
- reuse trusted narrative primitives across future contexts;
- reduce duplication and narrative drift;
- evolve presentation independently from product and financial execution authority; and
- test whether orientation improves user comprehension before expanding the surface.

The first implementation should therefore be treated as a **bounded orientation experiment**, not as the launch of a general marketing website.

## Product belief

A user encountering Hedgr should be able to understand, before requiring deeper product explanation:

1. why Hedgr exists;
2. that Hedgr is fundamentally concerned with financial stability and value preservation;
3. how Hedgr approaches stability at a conceptual level;
4. that risk, liquidity, and exit readiness remain material; and
5. what Hedgr can and cannot currently do.

The orientation surface should strengthen the canonical journey:

**Entry → Orientation → Stability → Insight → Safe action.**

It should not require crypto expertise or use DeFi as the primary explanation of the product.

## Highest-value uncertainty

**Can a reusable Hedgr orientation surface establish the correct stability-first mental model before a user encounters the product, without creating false confidence or implying unauthorised financial capability?**

This uncertainty should govern the first implementation and its evidence collection.

## Bounded outcome

Create one reusable, web-native narrative orientation surface derived from governed Hedgr narrative material and implemented within existing institutional and product boundaries.

The first surface should remain:

- static or otherwise operationally minimal;
- informational;
- clearly distinguishable from live financial capability;
- consistent with governed Hedgr brand and UX doctrine;
- derived from governed narrative rather than becoming an independent source of positioning;
- reversible; and
- measurable as a comprehension experiment.

## Explicit exclusions

This conviction does **not** approve:

- a general corporate or marketing-site programme;
- public launch or publication by implication;
- new product positioning;
- unrestricted rewriting of canonical narrative;
- financial execution;
- deposits, withdrawals, conversion, custody, allocation, or settlement;
- live financial data;
- provider or regulatory claims;
- lead-generation or growth machinery;
- new sensitive-data collection;
- CMS or unnecessary infrastructure;
- additional parallel product-refinement authority; or
- any widening of Class B or Class C authority.

## Evidence standard

Success is not that the page looks polished or that participants say they like it.

The experiment should determine whether participants more accurately understand:

- what Hedgr is;
- what problem Hedgr addresses;
- why stability is central;
- what material uncertainty or risk remains;
- what is informational or synthetic; and
- what actions are actually available.

Evidence should lead to an explicit disposition:

**Retain · Revise · Remove · Retest · Escalate**

If the surface adds reading without improving comprehension, creates crypto/yield-first interpretation, weakens risk or liquidity understanding, or causes participants to infer live financial capability, it should be revised or removed.

## Architectural conviction

The durable institutional asset should be the **governed narrative primitive**, not the individual About page.

Narrative content, provenance, presentation, and deployment should remain separable so that future authorised surfaces can reuse coherent Hedgr explanation without independently recreating the company's story.

The first web orientation surface is therefore a consumer of this capability, not its institutional source of truth.

## Stop conditions

Stop or escalate the affected work if:

- the rendered narrative begins creating new positioning rather than expressing governed narrative;
- publication is proposed without explicit publication authority;
- the surface implies live financial capability;
- risk, liquidity, synthetic-state, or execution boundaries become less clear;
- new data, provider, legal, regulatory, or security exposure appears;
- implementation expands into a general marketing-site programme;
- the work conflicts with active participant validation or narrative evidence;
- the implementation requires authority beyond its named Class A / Green envelope; or
- repo-native activation cannot be verified.

## Institutional intent

The purpose of this decision is not to create more collateral.

It is to make Hedgr's institutional narrative **more coherent, reusable, testable, and resistant to drift** while preserving the distinction between narrative, evidence, product truth, and execution authority.

**Preserve integrity at the boundaries; optimise reuse and learning inside them.**
