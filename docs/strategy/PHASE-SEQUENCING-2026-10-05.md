## 1. Priority rethink — do not disseminate yet

Do **not** release or disseminate the Sarah/Daniel research route yet.

Dissemination without an honest answer to *"is this what Hedgr shows?"* trains participants on a promise Hedgr cannot keep today. The Stability Engine does not yet surface computed notes on the product. Present-tense research framing that implies otherwise produces inconclusive evidence and burns a scarce reviewer pool.

Consequence: the §339 release gate, the release ticket, and participant sessions stay **held** until the foundation below is moving and research framing is honest against what the product actually does.

Build the **evidence that makes Hedgr fundable in 2027** (accelerator, grant, pre-seed readiness).

This order is the path of travel. Micro-experiments and advisory polish are secondary until the base exists.
