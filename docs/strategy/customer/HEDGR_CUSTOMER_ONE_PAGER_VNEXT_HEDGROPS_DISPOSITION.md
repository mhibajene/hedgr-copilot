## 2. Bounded evidence reviewed

The candidate was reviewed directly against the D-075 revision objectives and governance boundaries.

HedgrOps finding: the candidate now

- begins with the user's problem of financial unpredictability rather than Hedgr's institutional architecture;
- describes Hedgr first as a calm experience for understanding financial position, risk, and access;
- makes preservation subordinate neither to growth nor yield;
- introduces the Stability Wallet only after establishing human meaning;
- places “Financial Stability Operating System” as an explicitly internal broader-system term rather than the primary customer proposition;
- preserves mobile-first savers and volatile/cross-border earners as the intended audience;
- removes customer-facing use of “safe next action”;
- keeps liquidity and exit readiness central;
- clearly states that today's experience is research, informational or synthetic, and uses simulated information;
- expressly denies real customer-money movement and the material live capabilities that could otherwise be inferred;
- frames the CTA as prototype research rather than deposit, earnings, or market-launch solicitation.

These choices are assessed as consistent with Hedgr's stability-first product intent and the requirement that simplicity must not conceal risk, uncertainty, synthetic state, or execution boundaries.

## 3. Alignment with the MVP North Star

**HedgrOps assessment: aligned.**

The candidate expresses the central customer thesis in this order:

financial unpredictability → understand current position → understand stability and risk → understand liquidity/access → consider what is available next.

That preserves the substance of the canonical test journey without requiring a first-time reader to understand internal Hedgr terminology.

The narrative also keeps:

- stability before speculation;
- preservation before growth;
- visible risk;
- liquidity and exit readiness;
- calm comprehension;
- no requirement for crypto expertise.

This is assessed as consistent with the UX doctrine that trust is the primary interface, risk must be explicit, status should precede action, and calm should outrank urgency or conversion pressure.

## 4. Governance drift signals

## 5. Trust-surface coverage

**HedgrOps assessment: strong and sufficient for narrative acceptance, if Founder later confirms.**

The candidate gives the customer four important truths before asking for engagement:

1. what Hedgr is trying to help them understand;
2. that risk remains visible;
3. that liquidity and access matter;
4. that today's experience is synthetic and does not hold or move real money.

The section **“What is real today”** separates enduring product intent from current prototype truth and expressly excludes live custody, deposits, withdrawals, conversion, automated allocation, live yield routing, investment management, and financial advice.

Narrative acceptance would still not establish that target users actually understand or value the proposition. That requires user evidence through the governed validation process.

## 6. Evidence gaps / watch items

No remaining gap is assessed as material enough to block a later Founder acceptance decision.

Two items remain watch items rather than revision requirements:

**Financial Stability Operating System.**  
The term remains relatively abstract for a first-time customer. In vNext it appears only after the human proposition and is explicitly described as an internal identity for the broader system.

**Future capability sentence.**  
“Future capabilities will only be introduced when they have been responsibly developed, tested, and validated” is a governance-oriented commitment rather than a current capability claim. Future revisions should avoid strengthening this into a guarantee of safety, approval, or availability.

## 7. Risks / stop conditions

The principal remaining risk is over-explanation, not under-disclosure.

Future iterations should resist adding more institutional terminology, Stability Engine mechanics, protocol or asset detail, yield mechanics, provider names, country specificity without authority, or additional disclaimers that obscure the core proposition.

**Rollback while pending:** retain frozen Customer One-Pager v1.0 as the controlling customer narrative source.

**Stop condition:** reopen this recommendation if the candidate is materially altered in a way that strengthens financial claims, weakens synthetic-state disclosure, reduces liquidity/risk clarity, or changes the core customer thesis.
