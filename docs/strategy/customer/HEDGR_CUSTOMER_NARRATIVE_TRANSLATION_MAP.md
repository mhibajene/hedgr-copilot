## Boundary Checks

Before `dependable` is used as customer-facing replacement language, verify that the Canonical Story and Narrative Style Guide support its implication. If they do not, use a more interpretive formulation. This check does not reopen the definition of Financial Stability.

Every future use of this map must confirm:

- the source claim is still current;
- the capability statement matches `HEDGR_STATUS.md`;
- risk and uncertainty remain visible;
- design intent is not presented as achieved outcome;
- no translation implies custody, execution, regulation, market readiness, guaranteed preservation, guaranteed liquidity, financial advice, or public-category adoption;
- the intended audience is explicitly named;
- publication is separately authorized.

If a human translation cannot preserve the claim and boundary together, do not use it. Escalate for Founder / HedgrOps review.
